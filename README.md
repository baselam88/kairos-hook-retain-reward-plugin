# Hook -> Retain -> Reward Structurer

Structures a short or post: hook names the constraint (0-3s), retain stacks the cost of leaving it broken, reward pays off with one usable operating rule.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

On LinkedIn the hook lands inside the first ~210 characters. The reward IS the call to action; no comment-keyword bait.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Hook -> Retain -> Reward Structurer to help me: structures a short or post: hook names the constraint (0-3s), retain stacks the cost of leaving it broken, reward pays off with one usable operating rule — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
